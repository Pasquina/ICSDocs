This topic contains detailed information regarding the installation of ICS on your RAD Studio IDE. IDE is capable of supporting many versions of Delphi and C++. For this reason, installation can appear somewhat daunting and prone to error. The intention of this document is to clarify the steps needed for a successful installation and to minimize the time and effort required to get ICS up and running in your environment.

As you work through the installation and examples, keep in mind that there may be some variance in requirements necessary to achieve success; be certain you are using generally accepted good practices and techniques for your particular hosting environment.
